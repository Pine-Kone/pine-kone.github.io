Insanity Calorie Calculator

This spreadsheet started as an assignment for an organizational management class at BYU-Idaho. The assignment: set a goal for the semester, with a penalty if you missed it and a reward if you hit it. I picked completing the Insanity workout program as my goal, and built this spreadsheet to log every session and estimate the calories burned along the way.

📗 Download the Spreadsheet

About This Project

Log each Insanity workout as you complete it, and the spreadsheet estimates the calories burned for that session. It also leans on conditional formatting to do the tracking for you: cells highlight automatically based on criteria - like whether a day's workout was completed or missed - so you can see your progress toward that semester goal (and whether you're headed for the reward or the penalty) at a glance, without manually color-coding anything yourself.
