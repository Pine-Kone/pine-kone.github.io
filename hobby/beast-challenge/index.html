The Rules

  1. Everyone picks one main challenge that's hard - but possible - before December 31, 2026.
  2. Every challenge comes with pretend prize money. Hitting milestones along the way unlocks chunks of it; finishing the challenge unlocks the grand prize.
  3. Progress gets logged honestly. No skipping pages, no kicking on pull-ups.
  4. Side quests are allowed - and encouraged.
  5. We cheer each other on. Nobody wins unless we all try.
