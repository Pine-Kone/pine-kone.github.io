Childhood - Game Boy Color

The Shiny Tentacool That Felt Like a Myth

Tentacool

I was a kid playing on a Game Boy Color when I ran into my first shiny Pokemon, and at the time I had no idea shiny Pokemon even existed. Nobody had told me the color palette could just be different. Nintendo Power hadn't covered it, none of my friends had mentioned it - as far as I knew, every Tentacool looked the same.

Then a wild one showed up that didn't. It was a strange, differently-colored version of a Pokemon I already knew by heart, and I remember staring at the screen half-convinced I'd stumbled onto some kind of glitch, or a secret nobody else had ever seen. There was no internet search in my pocket back then to explain it away in five seconds. Just a kid, a Game Boy, and this odd little creature that felt like a piece of legend I'd found completely by accident.

I didn't even have a word for what I was looking at yet. It just felt like magic.
