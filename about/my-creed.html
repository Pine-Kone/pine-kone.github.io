My Creed

A living statement of the person I'm striving to be.

Why I Keep a Creed

As a teen, I remember sitting in the car with my Nana and her sharing how she had learned about writing down your own personal creed. Her instruction was simple. If there’s something you do well that you want to keep doing well, you write it into your creed. If there’s something you wish you did better, you write the desired actions you would like to have taken into your creed as if you were already doing them. And then you review the document daily, adding or subtracting to it as needed.

This is my attempt at that, organized around five areas I try to center my goals around.

Spiritual

I love and serve God, and I always remember Him. My heart and my desires are turned to my Heavenly Father. I strive to be Second Coming ready by developing and keeping righteous routines that help my lamp stay trimmed at all times. I strive to be worthy of the Spirit at all times. I am grateful for the Atonement of Jesus Christ, and I show my gratitude by repenting and turning to the Lord as often as needed. I prepare daily to exercise the priesthood, and I read frequently the Oath and Covenant of the priesthood D&C 84:33–44 — The Oath and Covenant of the Priesthood 33 For whoso is faithful unto the obtaining these two priesthoods of which I have spoken, and the magnifying their calling, are sanctified by the Spirit unto the renewing of their bodies. 34 They become the sons of Moses and of Aaron and the seed of Abraham, and the church and kingdom, and the elect of God. 35 And also all they who receive this priesthood receive me, saith the Lord; 36 For he that receiveth my servants receiveth me; 37 And he that receiveth me receiveth my Father; 38 And he that receiveth my Father receiveth my Father’s kingdom; therefore all that my Father hath shall be given unto him. 39 And this is according to the oath and covenant which belongeth to the priesthood. 40 Therefore, all those who receive the priesthood, receive this oath and covenant of my Father, which he cannot break, neither can it be moved. 41 But whoso breaketh this covenant after he hath received it, and altogether turneth therefrom, shall not have forgiveness of sins in this world nor in the world to come. 42 And wo unto all those who come not unto this priesthood which ye have received, which I now confirm upon you who are present this day, by mine own voice out of the heavens; and even I have given the heavenly hosts and mine angels charge concerning you. 43 And I now give unto you a commandment to beware concerning yourselves, to give diligent heed to the words of eternal life. 44 For you shall live by every word that proceedeth forth from the mouth of God. I make righteous sacrifices, like choosing to spend time in the scriptures over playing hours of videogames. I am active in the cause of Christ and His desire to bring my family to Zion. I make regular and consistent time for the Lord, especially when it comes to prayer, reading the scriptures, attending the temple, and serving others. In short, I move my life forward with faith in God and with love for my fellow men and women. To live the gospel completely is my mission.

Social

I am fiercely loyal to my wife, and I love her dearly. I love and serve others. I pursue emotional intelligence so that my relationships can be strong. I am actively involved in my relationships. When I am with my family, I am present. I'm not a husband or father who sits on his phone when his family is present. I am there for them. I strive to be the type of father that my kids can look up to. I am present at work. I am encouraging in my interactions with others.

Intellectual

I have developed, and am actively developing the intellectual tools and skills that will allow me to better serve the people around me. I am both a teacher and a learner. I seek the gift of the Spirit to "learn languages." I place that in quotes because I'm not only referring to my goals to master Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Arabic, Hebrew, Greek and Norwegian, but also the languages of accounting, SQL, DAX, M Code, Python and whatever other computer programming languages will help me reach my goals. I continually sharpen the saw to increase my professionalism.

Physical

I maintain a healthy weight. If I find my weight to be out of line, I make healthy choices like mindful eating and exercise to get back to a healthy place. I eat right. I choose foods that I know will do my body good. I keep track of my calories before or during the moment I consume them whenever possible, logging them as promptly as possible if I can't do it in the moment. I make time to sleep well.

I take good care of my physical and mental health. I don't pick at myself or put myself down. I encourage myself along.

Personal Management

I tithe and donate fast offerings generously and promptly. I live within my means. I'm committed to being free from debt, so that I can build long-lasting wealth, not only for me, but for my family. I continue to live the scout oath, law, motto and slogan, and I have woven them into my character.
