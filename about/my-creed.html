My Creed

A living statement of the person I'm striving to be - one I return to often and keep updating as I grow.

Why I Keep a Creed

As a teen, I remember sitting in the car with my Nana and her sharing how she had learned about writing down your own personal creed. Her instruction was simple. If there’s something you do well that you want to keep doing well, then you write it into your creed. And if there’s something you wish you did better in life, you write the desired actions you would like to have taken into your creed as if you were already doing them. And then you review the document daily, adding or subtracting to it as needed.

This is my attempt at that, organized it around the five areas I try to center my goals around. I come back to it often, and I expect it to keep changing for the rest of my life.

Spiritual

I love and serve God, and I always remember Him. I strive to be Second Coming ready by keeping my lamp trimmed at all times. I strive to be worthy of the Spirit at all times. I am grateful for the Atonement of Jesus Christ, and I show my gratitude by repenting and turning to the Lord as often as needed. I make righteous sacrifices, like choosing to spending time in the scriptures over playing hours of videogames. I am active in the cause of Christ and His desire to bring my family to Zion. I make regular and consistent time for the Lord, especially when it comes to prayer, reading the scriptures, attending the temple, and serving others.

Social

I love and serve others. I am emotionally Intelligent. I am actively involved in my relationships.

Intellectual

I have developed, and am actively developing the intellectual tools and skills that will allow me to better serve the people around me. I am both a teacher and a learner. I seek the gift of the Spirit to "learn languages." I place that in quotes because I'm not only referring to my goals to master Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Arabic, and Norwegian, but also the languages of accounting or various computer programming languages. I continually sharpen the saw to increase my professionalism.

Physical

I maintain a healthy weight. If I find my weight to be out of line, I make healthy choices like mindful eating and exercicsing to get back to a healthy place. I eat right. I choose foods that I know will do my body good. I keep track of my calories before or during the moment I consume them whenever possible, logging them as promptly as possible if I can't do it in the moment. I make time to sleep well.

I take good care of my physical and mental health. I don't pick at myself or put myself down. I encourage myself along.

Personal Management

I tithe and donate fast offerings generously and promptly. I live within my means. I'm committed to being free feom debt, so that Incan truly build long-laating wealth. I continue to live tbe scout oath, law, and motto.
