My Creed

A living statement of the person I'm striving to be - one I return to often and keep updating as I grow.

Why I Keep a Creed

My grandma once taught me about keeping a personal creed. The idea is that you carry it with you through life and update it as you go. When you notice something you do that you're proud of, you write it into your creed as a reminder to keep doing it. And when you notice something about yourself you wish were different, you don't write down the problem - you write down how you want things to be, phrased as though you've already become that person. That way your creed becomes a set of beliefs you're actively reaffirming for yourself, rather than a list of shortcomings.

That idea stuck with me, and it connected with something I later read in What to Say When You Talk to Yourself. The book makes the case that the words we repeat to ourselves quietly shape who we become - and that if we're deliberate about feeding ourselves honest, positive, present-tense self-talk, we can reshape the beliefs that steer our lives. A creed, kept this way, is really just organized, intentional self-talk.

So this page is my creed. I've organized it around the five areas I try to center my goals around. I come back to it often, and I expect it to keep changing for the rest of my life.

Spiritual

Love and serve God. Be Second Coming ready. Be worthy of the Spirit. Have my lamp trimmed. Righteous sacrifice for the cause of bringing my family to Zion. Make regular and consistent time for the Lord.

Social

Love and serve others. Be emotionally Intelligent. Be actively involved in my relationships.

Intellectual

Develop the intellectual tools and skills that will allow me to better serve the people around me. Be a teacher and a learner. Seek gifts of the Spirit to "learn languages" (in quotes because I'm referring to the language of accounting or programming languages, not just foreign dialects). Master the languages "in every way possible." Be professional.

Physical

Maintain a healthy weight, eat right, and sleep well.

Personal Management

Tithe and donate fast offerings generously and promptly. Live within my means. Be debt free. Build wealth.
