My Story

I was raised in small town Idaho. Growing up, I spent some time working on my grandparent's farm. Faith in God, hard work and honesty were the lifeblood of everything they did. As I got older, I found myself working for local farmers in pea patches, and grain and potato fields.

During those formative years of my life, I came across the following verses in chapter 34 of the book of Alma in The Book of Mormon: "Yea, humble yourselves, and continue in prayer unto him. Cry unto him when ye are in your fields..."

When I was young, I could see the direct correlation to my time working on the farm. Now those verses have taken on a different meaning as I apply similar principles to the data fields I help oversee.
